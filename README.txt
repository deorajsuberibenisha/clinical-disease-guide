CLINICAL DISEASE GUIDE
======================
A stand-alone, referenced disease reference for clinicians and students in Bhutan and South Asia.
Separate from Bhutan Clinical Reference. Version of 22 November 2026.

WHAT IS INSIDE
--------------
• 28 chapter pages covering 16 diseases, with separate adult, child and pregnancy pages where care differs:
  acute kidney injury, chronic kidney disease, diabetes (adult, pregnancy, child), tuberculosis, pneumonia,
  heart failure, asthma, acute coronary syndromes, cirrhosis, hypertension, stroke, COPD, gastroenteritis,
  sepsis, upper GI bleeding, and peptic ulcer / H. pylori.
• 39 working calculators and scores (also gathered on one Calculators page).
• 136 drug cards plus a drug library with Bhutan formulary and NEML 2025 levels.
• Every chapter reads Bhutan's Standard Treatment Guidelines and essential medicines list side by side with
  Indian and international guidelines, shows where they disagree, and cites its sources with links.

HOW TO OPEN
-----------
Open index.html in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox) on a computer, tablet or phone.
Fonts and images load from the internet (Google Fonts, Wikimedia Commons); text, drug cards and calculators work offline.

READING OPTIONS (the "Aa Reading" button)
------------------------------------------
• Looks: Classic, Minimal, Focus, Compact
• Tints: Auto, Paper, White, Sage, Night
• Text size, typeface, line width, spacing, and a floating contents panel you can show or hide
• Save as PDF from any chapter (A4, page numbers, tables kept intact)

IMPORTANT
---------
For qualified health professionals and students. This guide supports clinical judgement; it does not replace it.
Check every dose against your current local formulary before prescribing.
